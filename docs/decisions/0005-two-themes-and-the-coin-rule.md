# 0005: Two themes, and coins mean the chance only

**Date:** 2026-11-10.

## Decision
- **Deep is the dark theme and Daylight the light one.** Deep is navy-violet
  with yellow; Daylight is white with blue. A first launch follows the
  phone's own setting; the switch overrides it.
- **Coins mean the chance and nothing else**, and look the same everywhere
  they appear: the gold coin of Today's hero. Distance to the goal is a path
  with a stop at every tenth. Counts are plain numbers. This replaces the
  earlier plan (2026-11-05) to put coins on Food and Train: those tabs show
  counts, and a second meaning for coins is what this rule removes.

## Why
The owner asked for something "happy, energetic and lively" (2026-11-05);
navy and brass read calm but dull, and black and gold was worse (tried and
reverted on 2026-11-03). Both themes were judged as real screens on the
phone, with real-looking data, before either shipped.

Progress used ten round marks twice on one screen: the chance in its hero and
"2 of 10 coins" to the goal underneath. A symbol that means two things
teaches neither, so it now means one.

## What would change it
People misreading the coins in the stranger test, or a theme failing a
contrast check on a real phone in sunlight.
