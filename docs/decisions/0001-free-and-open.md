# 0001: Free and open source, no paywall

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Replaces:** the "free core + paid Plus" plan of 2026-10-30.

## Decision
Slim Chance is free and open source (AGPL). No paywall, no subscription, no
in-app purchases. The "Plus preview" label goes. AI features stay free and get
a per-person daily limit so their cost stays bounded.

## Why
- Money is not the goal. The goals are to help people lose weight calmly and
  honestly, and to learn to build with AI (RAG, MCP, agents, evals).
- A paywall cost more than it would earn at our size: store fees, payment
  plumbing, tax advice, and a public "trader" listing in the EU stores.
- "Plus" had become a list of features, not a reason to pay; one of them was
  labelled and it read as an empty promise.
- Paying for health help excludes the people who most need it.

## What would change it
Running costs that can't be covered by limits, donations or an open-source
grant (for example NLnet). Even then, safety features stay free.
