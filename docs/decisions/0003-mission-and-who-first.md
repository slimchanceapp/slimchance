# 0003: What Slim Chance is for, and who first

**Date:** 2026-11-05.

## Decision
- **Mission:** help people losing weight on their own see honestly how it is
  going and know what to do today: calmly, for free, in the open. And be the
  lab where we learn to build with AI.
- **Centre of the product:** a plan for today (what to eat, how to move),
  with tracking as its feedback. Until now it was the other way round.
- **Who first:** adults who have lost weight before and regained it, doing it
  alone, who log roughly and often wear a watch.
- **How we know it helps:** people still weighing in at week 4, and the
  share of them whose trend moved toward their goal by week 8. Sign-ups and
  opens are not the measure.
- **Guardrails:** no shame, never under the safe floor, numbers checked for
  honesty, no data sold, medication logged but never advised on.

## Why
In a year no stranger came back after a week, so "does it help?" has no
evidence yet. The strongest signal we have is that a concrete plan from the
coach changed a real week; the odds alone did not.

## What would change it
Week-4 and week-8 numbers from the stranger test that point elsewhere.
