# 0002: A coin is an honest chance, about the recent past

**Date:** 2026-11-05.

## Decision
- The ten coins mean one thing everywhere: a chance worked out from the
  person's own data. Never decoration, never points.
- Each tab answers its own question that way, or shows no coins: Today and
  Progress, the chance the trend is really going the goal's way; Food, the
  chance the week ends inside the budget; Train, the chance this week's
  training days get done.
- The words say what the chance is about: the recent weeks, not the future.

## Why
- A simulation of 9,000 people with a known true rate, realistic water swings
  that linger for days and up to 40% of mornings missed, put through the
  app's own estimator: the chance is within about one coin of the truth
  across the whole range (slightly cautious in the middle, slightly sure at
  the ends). The coins are honest about the question they answer.
- On the one long real history we have, the chance did not predict the next
  weeks: people react to their own numbers. So the coin must not read as a
  forecast, and a good chance needs a plan beside it, not just praise.

## What would change it
A calibration check (when we say 7 in 10, does it happen about 7 in 10?)
that fails on real data from the stranger test; or a forecast feature that
earns its own, separately checked numbers.
