**Short description:**

A free, open-source weight-loss app that shows the odds your weight is really
going your way, from a rough food log.

**Description:**

Slim Chance turns morning weigh-ins into a trend and shows the chance that it
is really heading toward your goal, as ten coins, instead of a daily number
that is mostly water. Your calorie budget is learned from your own scale and
your own log, so a rough log works and you are never asked to log more. Log
food by photo, label, a few words or a usual meal in one tap. On Android it
reads workouts, sleep, resting heart rate and HRV from Health Connect. An
optional coach plans your next days from foods you already eat and arranges
your own training sessions; the app checks every number. No ads, no
trackers, data stored in the EU, and you can export or delete everything.
