## 1. For people who hate logging food

**Title:** I built a weight-loss app for people who won't weigh their olive oil

Most calorie apps assume you log everything, perfectly, forever. I don't, and
I've never met anyone who does.

Slim Chance works with a rough log. You weigh yourself in the morning and log
food however you like (a photo, a few words, your usual breakfast in one tap).
It learns your real budget from your own scale: if what you log runs a bit
low every day, the target is set in those same units, so it cancels out. It
never asks you to log more.

Instead of the daily number, it shows the odds your weight is really going
your way, as ten coins. When the scale jumps a kilo overnight, it tells you
why from your own week instead of letting you panic.

Free to use, no ads, no trackers, open source. Android and web; iPhone soon.
I'd love honest feedback, especially if it doesn't work for you.

https://slimchance.app/about/?from=post-logging

## 2. For people who train with a watch

**Title:** A weight-loss app that counts the gym session your watch recorded

If your watch logs your workouts, you shouldn't have to type them in again.
Slim Chance reads Health Connect: a gym session your watch recorded as
"Overall fitness" counts as a training day, and every morning it turns last
night's sleep, your resting heart rate and HRV into one line: how hard to
train today, next to your own normal.

The weight side is calm on purpose: a trend instead of the daily number, the
odds you're really losing as ten coins, and a weekly calorie budget learned
from your own scale.

Free to use, no ads, no trackers, open source. Android (Health Connect) and
web. Honest feedback very welcome.

https://slimchance.app/about/?from=post-watch
