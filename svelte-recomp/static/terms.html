What Slim Chance is

Slim Chance is an app (at slimchance.app, and for Android and iPhone) and a set of tools AI assistants can use (at slimchance.app/mcp). From dated scale readings it works out a weight trend, the rate per week with its range, and the chance in 10 that the weight is really moving the way you want. It also reads watch numbers such as steps, sleep and resting heart rate beside the scale, and helps you log food and training.

What it isn't

  • Not medical advice, and not a medical device. It describes your own numbers; it doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent anything.
  • Not for everyone. The app is for adults, 18 and over. If you're under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, have or have had an eating disorder, or take medicine that affects your weight, plan any weight change with a doctor.
  • Not a promise. A trend and a chance describe the readings so far. They can be wrong, and they say nothing certain about the future.

  • An account is yours to delete at any time, in Settings. A trial without an email lives on one device only.
